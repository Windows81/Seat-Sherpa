Why Seat Sherpa

It's not ride-hailing — it's a real carpool. Drivers are already heading your way with empty seats, so you split the cost of a drive that's happening anyway instead of paying a markup.

🚗

A real carpool, not a markup

Drivers are already making the trip — you're just sharing gas and wear. Prices are capped at the IRS mileage rate split across the seats, so it's always fair and nobody's running a business off you.

⚡

Beats the bus, the train, and the airport

Direct and point-to-point in a real car — no depots, no transfers, no TSA lines. Cheaper than a train ticket, comfier than a bus seat, and for most of these routes there isn't even a direct flight.

🌱

One car instead of two

The drive was happening anyway — filling a seat means one less solo car making the same trip. The app counts the CO₂ each shared ride saves, so you can see the difference add up.

🪪

Verified, rated drivers

Drivers are phone-verified with a license on file, and both sides rate each other after every trip. Trust built on real rides, not anonymous reviews.

🔒

Secure, upfront payments

Driver-set prices shown in full before you book — no surge, no bidding. Paid securely via Stripe and only released to the driver once your trip starts.

🛣️

Carpool-lane & toll perks

A real carpool has real perks: with two or more of you, use HOV/carpool lanes to skip traffic, and with a FasTrak Flex, carpools get discounted tolls in Bay Area express lanes and on the bridges. (Most carpool lanes need 2+; a few, like the Bay Bridge, need 3+.)

How it works

From signup to first ride in under five minutes.

1

Sign up & verify

Create an account with your phone. We send you a one-time code to confirm it's really you.

2

Find or post a ride

Riders browse rides going your way. Drivers post their planned trips and set a seat price.

3

Book and go

Pay securely in-app, coordinate with your driver, and hit the road. The riders' cost share reaches the driver about 2 days later.
