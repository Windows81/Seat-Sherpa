# Seat Sherpa

I live in Southern California and have a research demo to present in San José in September 2026.  To potentially save costs, I wrote a post on r/rideshare asking about carpool options to San José.  Reddit user u/sherpa10014 sent me a response linking me to his website:

> $35 is a realistic number for Bay to LA runs runs listed on Seat Sherpa right now - rides pop up all the time (or you can post) and rides I've seen rides from $20-$55 for norcal to socal for what people have posted and ridden (38 complete trips since we launched last month for this exact Bay to LA run). Fair warning, this is self-promotion: it's a carpool app, and an alert on the route is the part that actually does the work for a four-day window, since it pings you when a driver posts any of those dates, https://seatsherpa.app/app?c=reddit.
> 
> Feel free to reach out if you have any questions during sign up - I will respond personally! www.seatsherpa.app

Upon navigating to the site, I was pleasantly surprised to discover that the client-side source code was:
1. unobfuscated,
2. unminified, and
3. full of comments

This is my chance!  The source code shall be mirrored on GitHub!
