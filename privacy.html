Seat Sherpa ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Seat Sherpa mobile application (the "App"), a peer-to-peer carpooling service available in California and Nevada. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, date of birth, profile photo.
  • Phone number — collected during signup and verified via SMS one-time code. Used to enable in-app voice communication between matched drivers and passengers.
  • Driver-only information — driver's license image and extracted name/date of birth, vehicle details (make, model, year, color, license plate, VIN), insurance card image, vehicle photos.
  • Ride information — origin and destination addresses, departure date/time, seat selection, messages exchanged in ride chats.
  • Payment information — collected and processed by Stripe Inc.; we do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Device information — push notification token, app version, operating system, anonymized usage events.
  • Location information — with your permission, we collect device location to help set pickup and drop-off points, find nearby rides, and understand where our service is used (aggregate demand analytics). You can grant or revoke location access at any time in your device settings.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud (SMS phone verification, driver's license review).
  • To match drivers with passengers, coordinate rides, and process payments.
  • To send transactional notifications (booking confirmations, ride reminders, message alerts).
  • To respond to support inquiries.
  • To keep the community safe — authorized Seat Sherpa personnel may review in-app messages, reviews, and profile content in response to user reports, or where we reasonably suspect a violation of our Terms of Service (for example harassment, threats, fraud, or other safety concerns). Message content is never used for marketing or shared with third parties, and access is limited to trained staff acting on a safety or enforcement need.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations (e.g., 1099 reporting for drivers via Stripe Connect, identity-verification record-keeping).

We do not use your information for marketing purposes. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. SMS messaging consent

During account creation, you provide your phone number and affirmatively consent to receive a one-time verification code via SMS by tapping "Send code." You may also receive transactional SMS reminders for upcoming rides. Standard SMS rates from your carrier may apply. You can opt out of further SMS at any time by replying STOP to any message. To re-opt-in, reply START. For help, reply HELP or email support@seatsherpa.app.

We do not share SMS-related consent, phone numbers, or message content with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Service providers

We use the following service providers to operate the App:

  • Supabase — account database, authentication, file storage.
  • Stripe — payment processing and driver payouts (Stripe Connect).
  • Twilio — SMS verification code delivery.
  • Apple and Google — optional sign-in providers, push notification delivery.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting. Crash reports include your account id and email address so we can identify and fix problems you actually hit.

Each provider processes data only as required to deliver their service to us and is bound by their own privacy policies and data-processing agreements.

5. Cookies, advertising & analytics

Our website (seatsherpa.app) uses advertising pixels and analytics tags: the Meta Pixel, the Reddit Pixel, and the Google Ads tag, plus Vercel Analytics for aggregate page statistics. These tools set cookies or similar identifiers and receive information about your visit — pages viewed, device information, approximate location, and ad-click identifiers (such as fbclid or rdt_cid) — so we can measure our advertising and reach people who may be interested in Seat Sherpa. We also record ad-click identifiers in our own campaign statistics.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA), sending this information to advertising platforms may be considered “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not knowingly share personal information of consumers under 16.

Your opt-out choices. Our website honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal: when your browser sends GPC, our advertising pixels are not loaded during your visit. You can also opt out with the platforms directly (Meta Ad Preferences, Reddit ad settings, Google My Ad Center), or email support@seatsherpa.app with the subject “Do not share” and we will honor your request. The App itself does not currently include third-party advertising SDKs.

6. Information sharing between users

To coordinate rides, your name, profile photo, ratings, and (after booking confirmation) phone number are shared with the matched driver or passenger. Phone calls placed through the App use your device's native dialer and the other party's phone number is visible to you. Drivers' vehicle details (make, model, color) are visible to passengers who book a seat; the license plate is revealed 2 hours before pickup. We do not share your information with users you have not been matched with.

Public ride listings. Posting a ride publishes limited listing information beyond the App so riders can find it: your first name, driver rating, route (departure and arrival areas and any listed stops), date and time, seat availability, and price. These fields appear on our public website (including seatsherpa.app/rides and route pages) and in third-party surfaces that list available rides, such as Apple Maps. Your contact details, exact addresses, license plate, and profile photo are not included in public listings.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete account) or by emailing support@seatsherpa.app.

When you delete your account, we erase your personal information — such as your name, profile photo, email address, phone number, and driver's license details — and permanently disable your login. Booking and trip records you created with other users are retained in an anonymized form (your name is replaced with “Former member”) so the other party keeps their own records and to meet tax, accounting, safety, and other legal obligations — generally for up to seven years.

After deletion we retain a minimal deletion record — your account id, the email address on the account, and any reason you chose to give — for fraud prevention, audit, and legal-compliance purposes.

Your in-app message threads are handled differently: when you delete your account, the other party is notified that you have left, and the conversation is removed within a few days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information. California residents have specific rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what we collect and the right to request deletion. California residents may also opt out of the “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as described in Section 5 — via the GPC browser signal or by emailing us. To exercise any of these rights, email support@seatsherpa.app.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest, role-based access controls, and regular security review. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, contact support immediately.

10. Children

Seat Sherpa is not directed to children under 18. All users must be 18 or older; drivers must also hold a current, valid driver's license. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the App evolves or as regulations change. Material changes will be communicated through the App or via email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact

Questions or concerns? Email support@seatsherpa.app.
