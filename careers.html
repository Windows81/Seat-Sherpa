Seat Sherpa

Help us fill every empty seat in California.

Seat Sherpa is the carpool marketplace for intercity trips — live in the App Store, Google Play, and inside Apple Maps next to Uber and Lyft. We're a small team moving fast, and as we scale we're looking for top talent and interns across engineering, growth, operations, and design.

There are no rigid job descriptions yet — that's the point. Tell us what you're great at and what you'd want to own. You'll work directly with the founder, ship things real riders and drivers use the same week, and see your work in the numbers.
