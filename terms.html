1. Nature of the Service

Seat Sherpa is a technology platform. The Service connects individuals (drivers) who are planning a long-distance trip in their own personal vehicle with other individuals (passengers) who would like to share the trip and contribute toward the driver's costs.

Seat Sherpa is NOT a transportation carrier, taxi service, ride-hail company, or Transportation Network Company. We do not own, operate, or dispatch vehicles. We do not employ drivers. We do not determine routes, set prices, or guarantee that any trip will occur. We facilitate introductions and payments between users; the trip itself is arranged between the driver and passenger.

Per-seat amounts paid by passengers are intended to reimburse the driver for a share of the actual operating cost of the vehicle (fuel, wear, insurance, depreciation), and are capped at a level no greater than the IRS standard mileage rate multiplied by the trip distance, divided by the number of passenger seats offered, treated as no fewer than four. This is a cost-sharing arrangement, not a payment for transportation services. Drivers do not earn a profit from rides posted on the Service.

Note on regulatory classification. The Service is designed to operate as a cost-shared carpool platform under California and Nevada law and is not currently registered as a Transportation Network Company. This classification is fact-dependent and may be reviewed by regulators. We reserve the right to modify the Service to maintain compliance with applicable law in any jurisdiction.

6. Processing Fees & Cost Sharing

6.1 Per-Seat Contribution Amount

Drivers set the per-seat contribution amount for the rides they post, subject to a maximum cap calculated by Seat Sherpa for each route. The cap is calculated as:

max_per_seat = (trip_distance × IRS_standard_mileage_rate) ÷ max(4, seats_offered)

This cap is intended to ensure that the total amount collected across all seats does not exceed the IRS-recognized cost of operating the vehicle for the trip. Seat Sherpa does not control or recommend individual seat prices; we only set the maximum permitted.

6.2 Passenger Processing Fee

Seat Sherpa adds a processing fee equal to 5% of the seat contribution to each booking. This fee is added on top of the seat amount and is clearly shown to the passenger as a separate line item before they confirm payment. The fee covers credit card processing, secure payment infrastructure, fraud prevention, customer support, and platform operating costs.

Certain bookings also include a flat booking fee of $0.99 (for example, bookings placed with promotional ride credits or on specially discounted rides). When it applies, the booking fee is always shown as its own line item in the price breakdown before you confirm payment — the total you see at checkout is the total you are charged. Refunds treat the booking fee the same way as the processing fee.

6.3 Driver Processing Fee

Seat Sherpa retains a processing fee equal to 5% of the seat contribution from each completed ride. This fee is deducted from the driver's payout and covers credit card processing, 1099 reporting through Stripe Connect, and platform operating costs. The driver's earnings dashboard shows the net amount after this fee — that is the actual amount transferred to the driver's bank account.

6.4 Fee Changes

Seat Sherpa may modify these fees with at least 30 days' written notice via email or in-app notification. Fees in effect at the time of booking apply to that booking.

6.5 Taxes

7. Cancellations & Refunds

Each ride is posted with a cancellation policy chosen by the driver:

  • Free cancellation — Passenger receives 100% refund if cancelled at least 2 hours before departure. No refund within 2 hours.
  • Moderate — Passenger receives 100% refund if cancelled at least 48 hours before departure; 50% refund if cancelled between 2 and 48 hours before; no refund within 2 hours.

When a driver cancels a ride, all passengers receive a 100% refund regardless of the policy, including the passenger processing fee.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe and typically appear within 5-10 business days. Processing fees on completed (non-cancelled) rides are non-refundable except where required by law.
